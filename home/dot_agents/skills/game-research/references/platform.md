# Platform Research

Identify games worth playing from the requested platform's library today,
including its original releases via emulation when practical. Ask which platform
only if missing; clarify boundaries such as Game Boy versus Game Boy Color when
they materially affect coverage.

## Selection

Use editorial lists and community discussions to discover candidates, then assess
current enjoyment and practical playability. Do not substitute historical impact
or collector value for reasons to play. Avoid mandatory publication quotas.

Require two independent favorable sources for each game in either ranked list.
Apply this threshold to recommendations, not discovery or version enumeration.
Disclose promising single-source finds in a short limited-evidence note if useful.
Ambiguous general game praise or praise of another platform's release does not
satisfy the threshold for this release. Praise unambiguously referring to a
platform-exclusive release does qualify. Two endorsements establish corroboration, not broad
community agreement; apply the shared evidence classifications separately.

Aim for 10–20 primary recommendations, returning fewer when evidence is weak.
Do not pad. Include single-player and local multiplayer candidates and disclose
requirements. Use exclusivity, unique platform content, and genre diversity to
distinguish otherwise close recommendations; do not promote a weaker game merely
because it is exclusive or represents a genre.

Produce two mutually exclusive ranked lists:

- Worth Playing on This Platform: worthwhile releases from the requested library,
  with a practical playing setup and any material conditions.
- Better Experienced Elsewhere: otherwise qualifying releases for which a
  specific alternative offers a clear overall improvement under stated criteria.

Check credible alternative implementations for shortlisted candidates rather than
auditing every port of every game. Name the exact preferred alternative and
support its gains and losses with comparative evidence. A substantially different
remake may complement rather than replace the original. Retain the original when
the preference is conditional or unresolved, explaining alternatives in notes.
Emulating the requested release is not itself a reason to move it elsewhere.

## Output

Begin with platform, research date, play assumptions, and inspected source counts
by editorial/community category. Put each ranked list in its own plain-text code
block. Repeat this pattern with consecutive ranks. Each entry intentionally has
two lines: a comment-style rank/title line for context, then one bare title line
for copying or import. The ✓ means recommended from this platform's library,
including via emulation; it does not mean every feature or setup was tested.
The → identifies a preferred alternative. Keep commentary and citations outside
the blocks:

```text
## Best <platform> Games Worth Playing on <platform>

# 1. <game title> ✓
<game title>

# 2. <game title> ✓
<game title>
```

```text
## Games Better Experienced Elsewhere

# 1. <original platform game title> → <preferred release/implementation and host>
<original platform game title>
```

If no alternatives qualify, state that briefly instead of inventing entries.
Never combine separate games with slashes; retain a slash if it belongs to an
official title. Resolve aliases before counting or formatting entries. Distinct
paired releases remain separate titles; explain material overlap in the notes
rather than merging their identities or transferring source counts between them.

After the lists, provide a compact table for every included game:

| Game | Editorial count | Community count | Community evidence | Exclusivity / platform significance | Why play today? | Version/setup verdict and caveats | Sources |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Use the shared exclusivity definitions in the platform-significance column. State
multiplatform or unknown where applicable; identify unique content separately
from whole-game exclusivity. Briefly highlight strong platform or family
exclusives in the overview, including former exclusives when useful. Keep these
annotations outside the copy-ready blocks so bare title lines remain unchanged.

Link each counted source directly or through source IDs mapped to linked pages.
Cite technical comparisons separately where needed. End with consequential
exclusions, limited-evidence candidates, and unresolved comparisons. Check counts,
rank order, release identities, duplicate entries, and practical requirements.
